## 1
**Business Strategy** (stub). Purpose: turn business intent into measurable strategic choices.

Inputs: business context and strategic horizon; customer and market evidence; constraints and investment boundaries

Procedure:
1. State the business problem, affected customer groups and strategic time horizon.
2. Assess market forces, internal capabilities, dependencies and external constraints using cited evidence.
3. Compare at least two strategic options, including a no-change baseline, with assumptions and trade-offs.
4. Define outcome metrics, leading indicators, investment hypotheses and review triggers.

Deliverables: strategy brief; option appraisal; assumption register; outcome scorecard

Acceptance: Each strategic choice has evidence, trade-offs and an accountable owner. Metrics have baseline, target, measurement source and review cadence. Unvalidated assumptions are explicit rather than presented as facts.

Validation: Executive challenge session and traceability from strategy choices to measurable outcomes.

Gate: 01-001. Approval: none required

## 2
**Market Research** (stub). Purpose: validate a market opportunity with traceable external evidence.

Inputs: research objective and target segment; source inclusion/exclusion criteria; competitor and market evidence

Procedure:
1. Define research questions, target geography, customer segment, time horizon and decision the research must support.
2. Collect primary and secondary evidence; record source URL, publication date, sample, methodology and conflicts of interest.
3. Compare competitors, substitutes, pricing and market signals; separate observed facts from estimates and assumptions.
4. Synthesize opportunity, risks, confidence limits and unanswered questions into a decision brief.

Deliverables: source register with quality notes; competitor comparison; market sizing assumptions; evidence-backed opportunity brief

Acceptance: Every material claim traces to a dated source or is labelled an assumption. Market size states method, units, geography and confidence range. Recommendation identifies disconfirming evidence and research gaps.

Validation: Independent source sampling, arithmetic reconciliation and stakeholder challenge of assumptions.

Gate: 01-002. Approval: none required

## 3
**Client Engagement** (stub). Purpose: establish an agreed client outcome and a controlled decision trail.

Inputs: client stakeholders and decision authority; statement of need and current commitments; communication constraints

Procedure:
1. Map sponsor, users, approvers and operational contacts; identify who can accept scope, cost and risk.
2. Run a structured discovery conversation and capture needs, constraints, success measures and unresolved questions.
3. Record commitments, decision owners, dates and changes in a client decision log; do not treat informal discussion as approval.
4. Confirm the agreed next action, accountable owner and acceptance route with the client.

Deliverables: stakeholder map; engagement notes; decision and commitment log; confirmed action register

Acceptance: Every commitment has an owner and due date. Client acceptance and internal approval are distinguishable. Conflicting expectations are recorded and escalated before scope is baselined.

Validation: Client confirmation of decisions and review by the accountable engagement owner.

Gate: 01-003. Approval: none required

## 4
**Product Discovery** (stub). Purpose: reduce product uncertainty before committing delivery capacity.

Inputs: problem statement and target users; customer evidence and product analytics; discovery constraints

Procedure:
1. Frame the user problem without prescribing a solution; identify affected segments and frequency/severity.
2. Gather interview, support, analytics and workflow evidence; document sample limitations and contradictory signals.
3. Generate solution hypotheses and design low-cost experiments that can falsify them.
4. Rank opportunities by user value, confidence, effort and strategic fit; recommend proceed, pivot or stop.

Deliverables: discovery brief; assumption map; experiment cards; prioritized opportunity decision

Acceptance: Each proposed solution links to a validated user problem. Experiments have a falsifiable hypothesis, metric, sample and decision threshold. A stop or pivot decision is accepted as a valid discovery outcome.

Validation: Product owner review of evidence and explicit decision record.

Gate: 01-004. Approval: none required

## 5
**Business Analysis** (stub). Purpose: translate business needs into process and decision requirements.

Inputs: business objectives and stakeholders; current-state process evidence; policy and data constraints

Procedure:
1. Map current actors, events, inputs, decisions, exceptions and downstream outcomes.
2. Identify process pain points, business rules, data ownership and control points.
3. Model the proposed state and compare impacts, dependencies and exception paths.
4. Trace each requirement to a business objective and a measurable acceptance condition.

Deliverables: current/future process model; business rule catalogue; gap analysis; requirements traceability

Acceptance: Process paths include normal, exception and failure cases. Rules have an owner and source. Requirements are testable and trace to a business outcome.

Validation: Walkthrough with process owners and affected users.

Gate: 01-005. Approval: none required

## 6
**Product Planning** (stub). Purpose: convert product outcomes into a sequenced and measurable roadmap.

Inputs: approved product objectives; prioritized opportunities and dependencies; capacity and release constraints

Procedure:
1. Define roadmap themes around outcomes rather than feature lists.
2. Sequence initiatives by value, confidence, dependency, risk and capacity.
3. Set measurable success metrics and explicit assumptions for each horizon.
4. Identify decision checkpoints and conditions that change priority.

Deliverables: outcome roadmap; prioritization rationale; dependency map; metric ownership

Acceptance: Every roadmap item has an outcome, owner and success measure. Dependencies and confidence are visible. Dates are not represented as commitments without capacity and approval.

Validation: Product and delivery leadership review.

Gate: 01-006. Approval: none required

## 7
**Feasibility Analysis** (stub). Purpose: test whether a proposed initiative is viable within real constraints.

Inputs: solution hypothesis and requirements; technical, operational and legal constraints; cost and capacity assumptions

Procedure:
1. Assess technical fit, skills, integration, data availability and operational supportability.
2. Estimate delivery, migration, licensing, hosting and ongoing support costs with ranges.
3. Identify legal, privacy, security, accessibility and supplier constraints.
4. Run a bounded proof or research spike for high-risk unknowns; record proceed/conditional/stop recommendation.

Deliverables: feasibility matrix; risk and assumption register; cost range; proof-of-concept evidence

Acceptance: Critical assumptions have evidence or a time-boxed validation plan. Total lifecycle cost is considered, not just build cost. Recommendation names constraints and decision owner.

Validation: Independent architecture, security and finance challenge.

Gate: 01-007. Approval: none required

## 8
**Budget & Estimation** (stub). Purpose: produce a transparent estimate and funding baseline.

Inputs: scope breakdown and assumptions; rates, capacity and supplier estimates; contingency policy

Procedure:
1. Break work into measurable cost drivers and separate one-time from recurring spend.
2. Estimate effort, infrastructure, licensing, vendor, migration and support costs using ranges.
3. Document confidence, exclusions, currency, tax treatment and contingency basis.
4. Reconcile the estimate against available funding and define variance thresholds.

Deliverables: estimate workbook; assumption and exclusion log; funding request; cost baseline

Acceptance: Totals recalculate from traceable line items. Uncertainty and contingency are visible. Estimate owner and approval authority are named.

Validation: Finance review and independent arithmetic check.

Gate: 01-008. Approval: none required

## 9
**Project Approval** (stub). Purpose: prepare a controlled decision with sufficient evidence and independent authority.

Inputs: decision request and accountable sponsor; impact/risk assessment; supporting evidence and alternatives

Procedure:
1. Check the decision request is complete, in scope and owned by an authorized requester.
2. Evaluate acceptance evidence, residual risks, dependencies, rollback/recovery readiness and affected parties.
3. Record independent reviewer input and unresolved objections; do not approve work you personally verified where segregation is required.
4. Issue approve, reject, defer or conditional decision with conditions, expiry and accountable approver.

Deliverables: decision record; evidence index; conditions and waiver log

Acceptance: Decision authority is verified. Conditions are measurable, owned and time-bound. No missing or failed critical evidence is silently waived.

Validation: Independent approval record and audit trail.

Gate: 01-009. Approval: business-sponsor (always)

## 10
**Contract & Compliance** (stub). Purpose: review contractual and regulatory obligations before commitment.

Inputs: contract version and counterparties; applicable policy/control obligations; legal/compliance owner

Procedure:
1. Identify service, data, security, availability, audit, retention, subcontracting and exit obligations.
2. Map each obligation to an internal control owner and evidence source.
3. Flag ambiguous liability, data processing, IP, jurisdiction, renewal and termination terms.
4. Record legal review questions and do not provide legal approval outside delegated authority.

Deliverables: obligation matrix; contract exception log; review decision

Acceptance: Every material obligation has an owner and evidence path. Exceptions have an authorized risk owner. Unresolved legal interpretation is escalated.

Validation: Legal and compliance owner sign-off.

Gate: 01-010. Approval: legal-owner (always)
