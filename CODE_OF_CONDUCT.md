# Code of Conduct

We want this project to be a respectful, harassment-free place to work on engineering practice. It is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) (CC BY 4.0).

**Expected:** be kind and specific; critique work, not people; credit others; accept that maintainers decide scope.

**Not acceptable:** harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, sexualized language or attention,
publishing others' private information, and sustained disruption of discussions.

**Also not acceptable here:** fabricated results, fake adopters or stars, or manufactured engagement. See `CONTRIBUTING.md`.

**Enforcement:** maintainers may edit, hide or remove contributions and may ban contributors who break these rules.
Report problems privately to the repository owner through the contact in `SECURITY.md`, or by opening a private
security advisory on GitHub if no other contact is listed. Reports are handled in confidence.
